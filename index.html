Illustration Creative Process

1. Moodboard

I first begin by gathering reference images from real life or online resources and place them in one collective moodboard on Miro.

2. Thumbnails and Sketch

Second, I come up with different thumbnail ideas to figure out the composition and simple colors of the piece.

3. Initial Sketch

After settling on the composition, I start sketching and laying down big flat colors on Photoshop.

4. Rendered Final

After long hours of painting and rendering, as well as finalizing the lighting, I'm finally done with my illustration.

About

Stacey Xu is a Chinese-American illustration student studying at the School of Visual Arts. Besides illustration, she also has background education in comics and game design. She is from the Silicon Valley, California. She likes using illustrations and comics to create characters, worlds, and stories in hopes of connecting with people. Her interests include: anime, manga, fashion, gaming, cats, and music. Whenever she is not hibernating in her room drawing, gaming, or watching anime, you can find her at cozy cat cafes sipping a matcha latte or dancing at a hyperpop rave past midnight.

Experiences

Experiences include doing free-lance commisioned illustrations and YouTube video thumbnails, formal art education, collaborating with game designers on designing games, making content for social media, building websites for local artists, and many more that can be found in the resume attached below.
